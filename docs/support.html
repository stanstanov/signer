How to Use Sivelo

  1. Tap Open to select a PDF document.
  2. Tap Signature to draw your signature with your finger.
  3. Tap anywhere on the PDF page to place the signature.
  4. Drag the signature to adjust its position.
  5. Tap Save to export the signed PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I place multiple signatures?
Yes. Open Settings and enable "Multiple signatures". Each tap will place a new signature.

Can I change the signature color?
Yes. In the signature pad, tap a color swatch or choose a custom color.

Does the app work offline?
Yes. Sivelo works entirely offline — no internet connection is needed.

Is my data safe?
Sivelo does not collect, store, or transmit any data. All documents stay on your device.
