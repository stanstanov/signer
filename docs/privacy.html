Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Sivelo ("the App") is developed by Turron. This policy describes how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

Sivelo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The App works entirely offline on your device.

PDF Documents & Signatures

All PDF files you open and signatures you draw remain on your device. The App does not upload, share, or send your documents or signatures to any server.

Analytics & Tracking

The App does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. No usage data is collected.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no data is collected from any user, the App is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at info@turron.lt.
